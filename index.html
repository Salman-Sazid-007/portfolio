01 About me

Curious engineer, product thinker.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from the University of Rajshahi, currently working remotely as a Mobile App Developer at Fuego.io, USA. For the past two years I've been shipping cross-platform applications used by real customers — from the first wireframe to the store release.

My sweet spot is e-commerce: product listings, carts, checkout flows and order management that stay fast and predictable under real-world conditions. I care about the small details — loading states, empty states, and interfaces that never make a user stop and think.

Outside of work I research climate modelling, where I combined Prophet and XGBoost on NASA POWER data to forecast long-term temperature trends for my undergraduate thesis. I'm currently looking for a software engineering role where I can keep building user-friendly products and grow with the team.
